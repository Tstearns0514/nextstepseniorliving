Senior Living Options

Finding the right level of support.

Senior living can mean very different things. Understanding the differences is one of the first steps toward making the right decision.

01

Independent Living

Maintenance-free living designed for older adults who want independence, convenience, activities, and community.

Best for older adults who are largely independent but want a simpler lifestyle and community setting.
02

Assisted Living

Support with daily activities while helping residents maintain as much independence as possible.

May include help with personal care, meals, medications, and other daily routines depending on the community.
03

Memory Care

Specialized environments and support for individuals living with Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia.

Designed around added structure, safety, specialized support, and dementia-focused programming.
