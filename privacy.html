Privacy Policy

NextStep Senior Living, LLC • Effective August 31, 2026

Please do not submit medical records, diagnoses, medication information, Social Security numbers, insurance information, or other sensitive health information through the public referral form.

Information We Collect

NextStep Senior Living may receive contact information and general referral information that you choose to provide, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, general senior-living preferences, care category, timing, relationship to the prospective resident, and other non-sensitive information.

How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, contact families or referral sources, discuss senior-living options, coordinate referral-related follow-up, and communicate about NextStep services.

Public Referral Form

The public referral form on this website is designed for basic contact and referral information only. It is not intended for medical records or other sensitive health information. Submitting the form prepares an email using the email application on the user's device. The website itself does not store the entered form data.

Sensitive Information

If additional medical, clinical, financial, or other sensitive information is needed, NextStep may provide separate instructions for an appropriate method of communication. Do not send Social Security numbers, insurance member numbers, complete medical records, or similarly sensitive information through the public form.

Information Sharing

NextStep may share information when reasonably necessary to assist with senior-living placement or referral coordination and when authorized by the individual, family, representative, or referring party. NextStep does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Service & Compensation

NextStep does not charge families for placement assistance. NextStep may receive compensation from participating senior living communities when a referred individual moves into a community.

Security

NextStep uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the information it handles. No internet or email transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact NextStep to ask questions about information you have provided, request corrections, or discuss privacy concerns.
