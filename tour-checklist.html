Senior Living Tour Checklist

Use this checklist to help compare communities based on the things that matter most to you and your family.

Community:
Tour Date:

First Impressions

Did the community feel welcoming and comfortable?
Were team members friendly and engaged with residents?
Did common areas and apartments appear clean and well maintained?
Could you picture yourself or your family member living here?

Care & Support

What level of care can the community provide today?
What happens if care needs increase?
How are emergencies and changes in condition handled?
How are medications and personal care needs managed?

Staffing & Communication

Who is the main point of contact for families?
How does the community communicate updates or concerns?
What staffing is available overnight and on weekends?
How does the community respond when a resident needs assistance?

Dining, Activities & Daily Life

Can we see a menu and dining schedule?
What activities are offered regularly?
How are new residents introduced to the community?
Are transportation and outings available?

Cost & Agreement

What is included in the monthly rate?
What services or care levels cost extra?
How often can rates or care charges change?
Are there entrance, community, or move-in fees?
