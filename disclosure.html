Service & Compensation Disclosure

Information about NextStep Senior Living's services, compensation, community relationships, and the choices available to families.

Our Services

NextStep Senior Living, LLC ("NextStep") assists seniors and their families in exploring senior living options, including Independent Living, Assisted Living, and Memory Care. Our role may include helping families:
  • Understand different senior living options and levels of support.
  • Identify communities based on needs, preferences, location, and budget.
  • Obtain information about communities, services, amenities, pricing, and availability.
  • Coordinate introductions, tours, and communication with communities.
  • Navigate the senior living search and selection process.

NextStep does not provide medical, nursing, legal, financial, or other professional healthcare services. Senior living communities are independently responsible for determining whether they can appropriately meet a prospective resident's needs.

No Cost to Families

Families do not pay NextStep for our senior living placement and advisory services.

NextStep is compensated by certain participating senior living communities when an individual referred by NextStep chooses and moves into that community.

How NextStep Is Compensated

If a family selects and moves into a participating senior living community, that community may pay NextStep a referral or placement fee pursuant to a separate agreement between NextStep and the community.

The family is not responsible for paying this referral fee to NextStep.

NextStep's compensation may vary among participating communities.

Participating and Non-Participating Communities

NextStep does not have compensation agreements with every senior living community.

Some communities presented or discussed may compensate NextStep if a resident moves in, while other communities may not have a financial relationship with NextStep.

The existence or amount of a referral fee does not obligate a family to select a particular community.

Your Choice Comes First

The final decision regarding where to live always belongs to the prospective resident and/or their authorized representative.

NextStep may provide information and recommendations based on factors such as:

  • Care and support needs
  • Budget
  • Geographic preferences
  • Lifestyle and personal preferences
  • Desired services and amenities
  • Availability
  • Information provided by the prospective resident or representative

Families are encouraged to independently evaluate communities, ask questions, review residency agreements and pricing, tour communities when possible, and determine which option is appropriate.

NextStep does not guarantee the quality, suitability, availability, licensing status, performance, or services of any senior living community.

Healthcare Services

NextStep's senior living placement services are separate from the selection of physicians, hospice providers, home health agencies, pharmacies, therapy providers, durable medical equipment suppliers, and other healthcare providers.

NextStep does not require a family to select any particular healthcare provider as a condition of receiving senior living advisory services.

Broker Services

NextStep does not broker, lease, or sublease apartments directly and is not a party to any transaction between landlords (including, as applicable, property management companies and/or property managers) and renters.

As a result, NextStep does not (a) guarantee or ensure any apartment or any transaction between a renter and landlord, (b) collect or process payments or execute any lease or sublease documentation on behalf of renters or landlords, or (c) broker, lease, or sublease or offer to broker, lease or sublease, or own any apartments.

NextStep Senior Living, LLC
Families are not charged for NextStep's senior living placement assistance. NextStep may receive compensation from participating senior living communities when a referred individual moves into a community. Recommendations and placement decisions remain your choice.
